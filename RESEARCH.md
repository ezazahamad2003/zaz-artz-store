## Seasonal

- **Christmas**: matching family pajamas and coordinated sweatshirts are the headline holiday category; "Mama Elf / Papa Elf / Tiny Elf" sets, pet-and-owner matching, and family superlatives ("Santa's Favorite") are the named best sellers (Imprint, Printful, Glossy). Built: Elf Squad, Family Name PJs, Santa's Favorite, Pet + Owner Matching, Ugly Sweater hoodie.
- **New Year**: group party-crew shirts and year-in-review photo tees. Built: Cheers to 2027 Crew (numbered backs), New Year Same Me, Year in Review photo tee.
- **Valentine's 2027**: matching couple outfits and photo-friendly gifts trending with Gen Z. The custom "bootleg girlfriend / boyfriend" Comfort-Colors-style photo tee shows an estimated 47,000 sales on a single Etsy listing (third-party estimate). Built: Bootleg Girlfriend, Bootleg Boyfriend, Est. Anniversary hoodies, Taken By.

## Anime and fandom

- Anime streetwear is now a mainstream fashion category; buyers favor clean character-inspired hoodies over giant prints. Series most cited for hoodies in 2026: Berserk, Jujutsu Kaisen, Chainsaw Man, Hunter x Hunter, Attack on Titan, with Dandadan and Dungeon Meshi rising. Global anime apparel market estimated around $4B (Meticulous Research).
- **KPop Demon Hunters** was the surprise merch phenomenon of 2025 with Mattel, Hasbro, Funko and Spin Master lines through 2026; **Stranger Things** final season drove retail drops at Target.
- Licensed characters and logos cannot be printed without a license, so the store sells the *aesthetic* instead: AI "Anime Me" portraits of the customer, chibi pets, kanji name tees, manga-panel photos, a logo-free 80s-town retro title treatment, a Korean folk-art tiger-and-magpie "idol era" graphic, and generic "No Spoilers" fandom tees. All are labeled fan-made and unofficial.
